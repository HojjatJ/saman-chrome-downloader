Privacy Policy – Saman Project File Downloader

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Data collection

None. This extension does not collect, store or transmit any personal or non-personal data to any server. It has no analytics, trackers or ads.

What it does

Only when you are on a project page at saman.fceo.ir and click "Start download", the extension reads the file links and form fields on that page and takes a screenshot of the info section. Everything is saved locally to your Downloads folder.

Credentials

The extension never reads or stores your username, password or cookies. Files are downloaded using your browser's own signed-in session.

Third parties

No data is shared with any third party.
